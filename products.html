The Fleet

Different sectors.
The same care.

Three specialised pilots, each shaped around the work of its sector. Business context, practical tools and AI assistance in one workflow, with you making the decisions that matter.

SYS-01

SabaiPilot

Online
Hostels & dormitories · Bed-by-bed operations

Your hostel pilot, built around dormitories and bed-by-bed operations. Bring reservations, bed availability and guest conversations into one workspace. Sabai uses your property's context to draft replies, giving you more room to welcome guests. You review and decide what to send.

Connected reservations — Bring Booking.com and Hostelworld reservations into one place through your Beds24 connection.
Bed-level availability — See availability for individual beds as well as rooms.
Guest conversations — Read and manage messages received through your Beds24 connection in a shared inbox.
Replies for review — Prepare replies to guest messages and reviews using your property's context. You approve them before sending.

SYS-02

PetraPilot

Online
Construction · Timber framing, roofing & zinc work · France

Your quoting pilot, built around your trade. Describe the job, and Petra proposes work items from your catalogue, with materials and labour together. Adjust the measurements, check the costs and confirm every line. Your experience shapes the final quote.

Your catalogue, your costs — Choose a work item and enter a measurement to prepare a costed line. AI assistance is optional.
From description to draft — Describe the job in plain language. Petra proposes quote lines for you to adjust and confirm.
Map-assisted estimates — Use an aerial building footprint and your roof-pitch input to estimate roof surface area.
Detailed quotes — Show materials and labour line by line, then print the quote or save it as a PDF.
Visit PetraPilot ↗
SYS-03

CircePilot

Invite-only
Marketing & content · Across sectors

Your brand's marketing pilot. Build a Brand Brain around your business, then work with Circe on email and social content. A shared foundation keeps your voice and business context close to each draft. You refine the work and decide what to publish.

A brief to guide Circe — Set your goal, then review drafts prepared from your Brand Brain.
Your Brand Brain — Build shared brand context from your website, YouTube content or a guided questionnaire.
Content for each channel — Draft email, Instagram and LinkedIn content from the same brief, with a version suited to each channel.
Your decision to publish — Review and edit each draft, then publish or send it with your usual tools.
