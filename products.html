SYS-01

SabaiPilot

Online
Independent hospitality · Southeast Asia

Hostel and guesthouse owners run their bookings across five different platforms, in five different tabs, with no single view of who’s arriving, who needs a reply, or which bed is actually free. SabaiPilot centralizes all of it — reservations, messages, reviews and inventory — into one system built around how independent hospitality actually works.

Channel sync — Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, Hostelworld and Trip.com reservations, centralized automatically.
Bed-level inventory — tracks availability down to the individual bed, not just the room.
One inbox — every guest conversation, across every platform, in a single place.
AI-drafted replies — Sabai drafts responses to messages and reviews. It never sends on its own — a human always approves first.

SYS-02

PetraPilot

Online
Construction · Charpente, Couverture, Zinguerie · France

Same method as SabaiPilot: sector first, software second. Scoped directly with an industry professional — fifteen years running a carpentry business, now in sales — PetraPilot builds a devis (quote) the way the trade actually quotes it: materials and labor bundled per work-item, not generic line items. A catalog-driven engine does the job with zero AI required; AI accelerates the parts worth accelerating — proposing devis lines from a plain-language site description, or assisting the on-site measurement from aerial cadastre data — with a human confirming every line before it counts.

Catalog-driven quoting — pick a work-item template, enter the measurement, get a costed line. Works with zero AI.
AI-proposed lines — describe the job in plain language, review the proposed devis lines, confirm what’s right.
Map-assisted survey — aerial building footprint plus roof pitch, corrected to real surface area automatically.
Fully itemized PDF — every quote breaks down materials and labor line by line, a gap in the tools it replaces.
69€HT / month or 690€ HT / year

SYS-03

CircePilot

Invite-only
Cross-vertical · Marketing & content automation

Different shape from the other two: not one sector, but any online business. A brand talks to a single agent, Circe — the specialized work behind her (writing, measuring, refining) stays internal, never surfaced as a catalog of tools. A Brand Brain, built from the business’s own website, YouTube channel and social content — or a guided questionnaire for a brand starting from scratch — becomes the single source of truth every piece of content is generated from, so tone and facts stay consistent instead of drifting post to post.

One agent, Circe — every conversation happens with a single visible agent, not a catalog of modules.
Brand Brain — ingests the business’s own site, YouTube and social presence, or a guided questionnaire, as the one source every generation reads from.
Multi-channel content — email, Instagram and LinkedIn today, each written fresh from the same brief, not reformatted from one channel into another.
Human always approves — Circe proposes copy and content. She never posts or sends on her own.
Piloting with real brands, not yet commercialized — invite-only while the product is shaped around real usage.
