BP–05 / The Grimoire

Learn to work with an AI crew.

The Grimoire is an interactive visual playbook for working with AI over the long run: not tricks for one good answer, but habits that make the collaboration last. In English and French. You need no installation to read it.

The promise

The promise, from your side

Most of us learned to use AI one question at a time. It works, until the work gets bigger than a conversation.

The Grimoire is about the part that comes after: how a person and an AI crew actually keep working together. You bring the intent and the judgement. The crew brings proposals and effort. The Grimoire shows how to make that partnership dependable.

Who it’s for

  • You use an AI assistant regularly and feel there should be a better way to work with it.
  • You are an independent, a founder, or someone curious about working with AI beyond one-off questions.
  • You are a beginner or not: no technical background is required.

Who it’s not for

  • If you want a catalogue of tricks for one-off answers.
  • If you expect a certified course or a guaranteed skill.
  • If you want a tool that does the work for you: the Grimoire is about how you work.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is The Grimoire available?

Not yet. It’s in preparation, with no date and no price. Join the Blueprints waitlist to hear when it moves forward.

What do I need to know beforehand?

Nothing technical. If you have used an AI assistant a few times, you are ready.

Does it depend on a particular AI tool?

No. It is written to be useful with the AI assistant you already use.

Does it need an account, or collect my data?

No. It is a self-contained guide. Reading it needs no account and no connection to an AI service.

What do you do with my email address?
