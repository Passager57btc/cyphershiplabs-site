GOOD TO KNOW

A few useful answers

Is the Grimoire available now?

Yes. The Grimoire is available to buy and download. The founder price is US$29 for the first 50 buyers, then US$39.

What do I receive?

A downloadable interactive guide, with six chapters, examples, exercises, copyable starting points and a mission workshop. English and French are included. This is a practical guide, not a video course or a technical encyclopaedia.

Can I read it offline?

Yes. Unzip the download and open START.html in a modern browser. Reading needs no AI service or account. Practising with an online AI assistant uses your own access to that service.

Does it store my notes or answers?

The guide requests no private text. Optional storage, off by default, remembers only language, reading checkmarks and workshop choices on your device.

Will there be an Ultimate edition?

A richer Ultimate edition is planned separately for later. A discount is planned for early buyers; its amount, content and release date have not been announced. It is not included in this purchase.
