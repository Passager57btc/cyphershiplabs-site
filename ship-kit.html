BP–02 / Ship Kit

One place for your projects, decisions and handoffs.

The Ship Kit is a personal workspace where your crew, your missions and your decisions live together, so you can pick up tomorrow exactly where you stopped today. It runs on your own computer. It is built on the Crew Kit foundation.

The problem

The problem, from your side

Your projects are scattered. A note here, a decision there, a conversation you can’t find. Working with an AI crew makes it worse before it makes it better: more threads, more moving parts, and no single place that says where things stand.

The Ship Kit is that place: one workspace, kept simple on purpose.

In the kit

What’s inside

01

A ship of your own

Name your ship, say what it is for, and start from a clean workspace. Nothing from our own setup comes preloaded.

02

Your crew, described

Give each crew member an identity, a specialty and a short introduction. A profile is a clear role on paper, not an agent running behind it.

03

Missions and decisions

Prepare a mission, follow its steps, and record each decision with the reason behind it, so you remember why, not just what.

04

Handoffs that hold

Keep notes, write a structured handoff at the end of a session, and come back later to find everything where you left it.

It’s not a hosted service and not an app store product. It’s a workspace you run yourself, with a written guide to go with it.

Scope

What it is, and is not

The kit IS

  • a workspace for one person, running on your own computer;
  • a place for your crew profiles, missions, decisions, notes and handoffs;
  • your data, stored on your machine, with an export you can keep;
  • a written guide that walks you through the first use.

The kit IS NOT

  • a team tool: it is single-user, with no accounts and no sharing;
  • a hosted service: nothing is stored on our servers;
  • a set of working agents: no AI runs inside it, and the statuses are yours to set;
  • finished: this is the first edition, and we will say what it does and does not do.

Who it’s for

  • You work on several projects and lose the thread between sessions.
  • You already work with an AI assistant, or plan to, and want one place that keeps the human decisions.
  • You are comfortable running a small program on your own computer by following a guide.

Who it’s not for

  • If you need a shared workspace for a team.
  • If you want something that runs agents for you: this organises the work, it doesn’t do it.
  • If you want an installer with one click: a short setup is part of the experience, and the guide explains it.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the Ship Kit available?

Not yet. It’s in preparation, with no date and no price. Join the Blueprints waitlist to hear when it moves forward.

What do I need?

A computer you control, a recent version of a free, widely used runtime (the guide tells you which and how to get it), and the patience for a short first setup. You do not need to be a developer.

Where is my data?

On your computer, in a folder you choose. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. You can export everything as a backup. For now, the export is a backup to keep, not a one-click restore.

Is it connected to an AI?

No. The first edition doesn’t connect to any AI service. It organises your work; it doesn’t run agents.

What do you do with my email address?

It’s used only to tell you when the Blueprints you chose are out. It’s processed by Brevo (EU hosting), never sold, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Details: privacy policy.
