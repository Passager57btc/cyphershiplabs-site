The problem

The problem, from your side

Typing is slower than thinking. And when you finally dictate, the transcription trips on exactly the words that matter: your project names, your clients, your jargon.

We built a dictation tool for ourselves, with a lexicon that fixes those words. The Transmission Kit is the effort to make that approach something you can follow and make your own.

Scope

What it is, and is not

The kit IS

  • a guide and a prototype for dictating to your AI crew;
  • built around a lexicon you choose and send on purpose;
  • Windows first.

The kit IS NOT

  • verified on every system: the Windows version has not yet been tested on a real Windows computer, and we will say so until it has been;
  • a one-click installer or a polished application;
  • a fully private tool: your audio goes to an outside transcription service. We make no claim about how that service keeps data, and the guide says so.

Who it’s for

  • You think faster than you type, and you already work with an AI assistant.
  • Your work has specific words (names, brands, terms) that ordinary dictation gets wrong.
  • You can follow a technical guide and are happy to be among the first to try a prototype.

Who it’s not for

  • If your work requires that audio never leaves your computer.
  • If you expect a polished, installable app today.
  • If you need guaranteed accuracy: transcription can still make mistakes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the Transmission Kit available?

Not yet. It’s in development, with no date and no price. Join the Blueprints waitlist to hear when it moves forward.

Which computers does it work on?

Windows is the first target, and it is not yet verified on a real Windows machine. Other systems are not covered at this stage. We will tell you plainly when each one has actually been tested.

Where does my voice go?

Your audio is sent to an outside transcription service, along with your lexicon if you choose to provide one. A temporary audio file is kept on your computer while it works and is normally deleted afterwards. We do not promise anything about the service’s own retention policy.

What do I need?

A microphone, a computer, an account with the transcription service, and a little comfort with a technical guide. The exact list will be published before anything goes on sale.

What do you do with my email address?

It’s used only to tell you when the Blueprints you chose are out. It’s processed by Brevo (EU hosting), never sold, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Details: privacy policy.
