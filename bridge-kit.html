BP–03 / Bridge Kit

A voice. A physical presence.

The Bridge Kit is a practical path towards a small robot that talks with you and remembers your work, connecting your AI crew to a device you can see and hear. It is a guide and a configuration, built from the setup we use ourselves, not a robot in a box.

In development. Not for sale yet: you can join the Blueprints waitlist to hear when the Bridge Kit moves forward. No date, no price.

The idea

The idea, from your side

Working with an AI usually means a screen. We wanted something closer: a voice that answers, a small device on the desk, and a memory that carries from one conversation to the next.

It works for us. The Bridge Kit is the effort to turn our own setup into something you can follow, without pretending it is simpler than it is.

In the kit

What’s inside

01

A guided path

A written, step-by-step guide that takes you from the idea to a first spoken exchange, with the reasoning behind each choice.

02

A configuration to adapt

A starting configuration for the software side of the link between your AI crew and the device, to adapt to your own setup.

03

Memory that carries over

The same persistent-memory approach as the Crew Kit, so the robot picks up your work instead of starting from zero each time.

04

Clear limits, up front

What leaves your machine, what stays local, and what you need to check before you trust the result.

It’s a guide and a configuration. It is not a finished piece of software, and it does not include a robot.

Scope

What it is, and is not

The kit IS

  • a guide and a configuration for connecting an AI crew to a compatible small robot;
  • built from a real, working personal setup, which serves as our first reference;
  • explicit about what runs on your computer and what goes to outside services.

The kit IS NOT

  • a robot: you buy the device separately, and none is included;
  • a promise of compatibility: we do not claim it works with any robot, computer or system;
  • a finished product: the generic software link is still being prepared and tested, and the kit will say exactly where it stands when it is ready.

Who it’s for

  • You like building: a little patience for a hands-on project is part of the deal.
  • You already work with an AI assistant and are curious about giving it a voice.
  • You are happy to buy a compatible small robot yourself and follow a careful guide.

Who it’s not for

  • If you want a ready-made robot that works out of the box.
  • If you need a guaranteed result on a specific model: we cannot promise that.
  • If you are not comfortable with spoken audio being handled by an outside service (see the FAQ).

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the Bridge Kit available?

Not yet. It’s in development, with no date and no price. Join the Blueprints waitlist to hear when it moves forward.

Does it include a robot?

No. You choose and buy a compatible device yourself. We don’t sell one, and we don’t claim the kit works with every model.

What do I need?

A compatible small robot, a computer, accounts with the outside services the guide names when it is ready, and some comfort following technical steps. The exact list will be published before anything goes on sale.

Where does my voice go?

Speaking to the robot involves an outside transcription service, which receives your audio, and an AI provider, which receives the resulting text. Speech output can be produced locally. The guide will show this chain plainly, so you decide with full information.

What do you do with my email address?

It’s used only to tell you when the Blueprints you chose are out. It’s processed by Brevo (EU hosting), never sold, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Details: privacy policy.
