## Prompt exact

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: premium CypherShip Labs website hero illustration on a transparent background, landscape 1536x1024.
Reference image 1: the canonical ship proportions and construction (turnaround). Reference image 2: material, identity and cinematic appearance (two states of same ship). Create ONE ship only, a refined cinematic 3/4 front-left view, nose pointing toward the lower left and engine toward the upper right, entire silhouette in frame with generous transparent margins.
Faithfully keep the slightly elongated dark navy civilian exploration hull, turquoise windshield band and warm copper engine and accents, complex but restrained realistic panelwork, small two-seat dorsal observatory on its telescopic mast, retracted state with pod seated low on the roof. NO side arm. NO logo. The ship should be recognizably the same design as these references, no new fins, weapons, wings or exaggerated antennae.
Rich but soft studio-cinematic lighting, crisp believable materials, a cool turquoise rim and warm copper interior windows, polished premium science-fiction visual. Exterior should be legible when placed on a navy-black starfield. No starfield or backdrop baked in: genuinely transparent background, no shadow rectangle, no typography, no labels, no other ship, no asteroids. This is an atmospheric marketing illustration, not a new technical turnaround.
