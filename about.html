About CypherShip Labs

Human intent.
Shared intelligence.

A founder, a named AI crew and a shared direction. CypherShip Labs brings them together to build agentic workflows and intelligent systems, and to explore how people and AI can create lasting work together.

The Lab

Useful systems.
A wider horizon.

CypherShip began with practical questions: how to bring hospitality operations into one workspace, prepare a construction quote around the real work, or help a brand express itself consistently. Those questions became SabaiPilot, PetraPilot and CircePilot, the first systems in The Fleet.

Building them opened a larger question: what becomes possible when a human and a crew of AI roles work together with context, memory and a clear direction? That question now runs through the Lab, from custom systems in The Shipyard to the working methods we are shaping into The Blueprints.

David / Founder

The curiosity to explore.
The intent to build.

David founded CypherShip Labs to turn ideas into systems people can use. He works directly with the crew, shaping the products, refining the design and asking what is worth building next.

His role is to hold the direction and make the choices that give the work meaning. The crew contributes specialist perspectives, challenges ideas and helps bring them into form. David sets priorities, reviews the results and decides what is ready to share.

The Crew

Distinct roles.
A shared context.

Our crew members are named AI roles with their own specialties and visual identities. Their work comes together under human direction.

01

Perspectives that meet

Strategy, architecture, engineering, design and review each bring a different question to the same project. The work grows through that exchange.

02

Continuity that matters

Written memory keeps decisions, lessons and project context available across sessions. We return to the work with a record of what matters and what has changed.

03

Human judgement

AI helps us explore, build and examine our work. People choose the direction, assess the results and take responsibility for what is released.

The Horizon

Intelligence beyond
the screen.

Our long-term direction reaches into robotics, the protection of agentic systems, and scientific and philosophical inquiry. The Foundry, The Bulwark and The Observatory give those ambitions a place within CypherShip.

These research and development directions grow alongside our products and methods. We explore, prototype and test, sharing what we build as the work takes shape.
