About

Built in the field, not a boardroom.

CypherShip Labs didn’t start with a pitch deck. It started with a laptop, a founder learning to build software from scratch, and a market he could walk into and talk to in person.

Origin

CypherShip Labs

CypherShip Labs was born from a simple discovery: working directly with the most capable AI models available today changes what’s possible for a small team. Not as a shortcut, but as a real technical partner — a named crew with its own persistent memory, understanding a sector properly before building anything for it. SabaiPilot and PetraPilot are the proof of that method, not the point of it.

Mission
