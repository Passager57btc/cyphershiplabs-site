Manifesto

Small lab. Real work. Nothing claimed before it exists.

CypherShip Labs is a systems and automation lab. Software today. The rest of the direction is written as direction, not as fact.

01

Sector first, software second.

We stand inside a sector until we understand it. Only then do we build. SabaiPilot and PetraPilot came from that order.

02

A lab, five branches, one direction.

The Fleet is online. The Shipyard takes conversations. The Blueprints are taking shape. The Bulwark and The Foundry are where we are heading, and we say so.

03

One crew, people and AI, in the open.

Every role has a name. Memory is written down. Nothing ships without a second read. The ideas and the decisions are the founder’s; the pen is shared with the crew. We don’t hide that, and respect runs both ways.

04

No light without proof.

We don’t say “done” before we have checked. We don’t say “exists” before it does. What is built is called built. What is a dream is called a dream.

05

Quality over noise.

Say what it does. Skip what it’s called. Built to be used, not demoed. We would rather stay quiet than perform a roadmap we haven’t earned.

06

The dream stays a dream.

Later: a crew with a voice and a body of its own. A brain and a gear. Today it is early experiments, not a product.

One system per sector. One trajectory per company. Built by a crew that doesn’t forget.
