Agents you can touch.

The world has enough software. What it lacks is presence.

CypherShip Labs builds intelligent systems: a body, a mind, and the skills of a trade. Agents and robots that listen, answer, act, and that you can keep within reach. We are a crew, aboard Calypso. We have a heading. We don't hide it.

01

Build, don't talk.

We would rather show what works. The rest needs no noise to exist.

02

One agent, one object, one service.

Not one more app. An agent with a face, a body, a voice, doing one specific job, every day.

03

A trade, and its skills.

Intelligence is not enough: you have to know how to do the work. Every trade gets its own agentic workflows, its own knowledge, its own tools. A hotel front desk, a workshop, security: each has its own.

04

Humans keep the intent. And the last word.

The agent carries out, suggests, warns. The person decides. Always.

05

A crew, with names.

Every agent has a role, a memory, a signature. No anonymous black box: colleagues you get to know.

06

One agent, many bodies.

Today, a conversation. Tomorrow, an object on a table. One day, a humanoid that welcomes you. The same agent, the same memory, just a new body. Gaspard is the first.

07

Robots to help, to protect, to rescue.

Never to harm. Protect first: our first line and our last.

08

Make it with our hands.

Parts we design and make ourselves, assembled electronics, tests run again and again. What we sell, we have held.

09

Start from one city. Go far.

We begin where we can check everything, street by street, client by client. Then we extend the course, city after city, across borders.

10

Dream big. Tell the truth.

What is built, we show. What is in the works, we say. What is a dream keeps the name of a dream. We aim far, and we don't need to lie to do it.

Space is vast, and it belongs to those who build. Come aboard.

— CypherShip Crew
